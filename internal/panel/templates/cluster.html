How to restore a tenant from its backup

A restore puts a backup in place of a tenant's data, on the node that runs it. The panel does not do it: it needs the backups' private key, which stays off the cluster. On that node:

sudo murder backups list {{$d.Tenant}}
sudo murder tenant archive {{$d.Tenant}}
sudo murder backups restore {{$d.Tenant}} -at 2026-11-01T18:50:55Z -identity key.txt
sudo murder tenant restore {{$d.Tenant}}

Archiving stops it while its data is replaced; what it had stays aside, unmounted, until you remove it. Pick the time from the list.
