Each wave moves once the one before has stayed healthy: two minutes for canaries, a minute after. A tenant not healthy within 5 minutes of its move pauses the rollout; you can then resume, abort, or restore their data.

Its data

Each tenant keeps its data as it moves, and a snapshot of it is taken first. A new image may change that data as it starts (a migration): going back to an older revision later does not undo it. Restore data snapshot, on the app's page while the rollout runs, puts the snapshot back, losing what was written since.
